AI product manager interviews: what the job asks for

You do not need to train models, but you must reason about them. This track covers the AI concepts a product manager is probed on, how to frame, spec, evaluate and launch AI features, and a 6-week plan of 3 hours on weekdays and 5 on weekends.

1What AI PM job descriptions ask for

This is a synthesis of requirements that recur in AI product manager postings, not a statistical count. Check it against the three or four postings you are actually targeting and adjust the weights.

TierSkill or topicWhere it shows up
Must knowAI product sense: when AI is the right solution, and which kind (rules, classic ML, LLM)Case questions: "would you build this with AI?" and use-case prioritisation
Must knowLLM working knowledge: tokens, context, prompting, RAG, agents, tool useTechnical rounds with engineers; you must be credible, not hands-on deep
Must knowEvaluation: golden sets, quality metrics, error analysis, online experiments"How do you know it works?" is the standard AI PM question
Must knowWriting specs for non-deterministic features: PRDs with eval criteria and failure handlingProduct-sense and execution rounds
Expected at senior levelAI UX and trust: uncertainty, citations, fallbacks, feedback loops, latencyDesign-partner and product-design rounds
Expected at senior levelEconomics: cost per request, latency, model choice, build vs buy, pricingStrategy rounds and cross-functional trade-offs
Expected at senior levelSafety, privacy, governance: prompt injection, PII, regulation, red teamingWorking with legal, security and trust teams
Expected at senior levelStrategy and leadership: moats, roadmaps under uncertainty, stakeholder communicationGroup and lead PM roles
Know the nameFine-tuning, MCP, vector databases, model routing, distillationOccasional; one-line explanations are enough

Written from general knowledge of the role, not from a fresh scrape of postings. Treat the tiers as a starting point.

2Concepts at interview depth

Each card has the few points interviewers probe and a line you can say out loud. Skip anything not here.

3Tools: one line each

Know what each is for and when you would pick it. Use means do it hands-on in the plan; Know means a one-line explanation is enough.

46-week plan

Weekdays are 3 hours and weekends 5 hours. Weekdays are for reading and short exercises, weekends for the project and mock interviews. One project ties the track together so you can say "I shipped this spec". Select a week to jump to it. Ticks are saved to your account.

Project sliceWhat you produceWeek
Problem and PRDOne AI feature for a support or knowledge assistant: problem brief, success metrics, PRD with eval criteria1 and 6
PrototypeWorking prompt and a grounded answer flow over a small document set, in a chat tool or no-code builder1 to 2
Quality30-case golden set, a scoring rubric, a run in promptfoo or LangSmith, and an error-analysis table3
Trust and safetyFailure-state designs, a 10-prompt red-team log, a risk and mitigation list4
Economics and launchCost-per-request sheet, build vs buy note, rollout plan with guardrail metrics5 to 6

Short on time: skip the fine-tuning and MCP notes and one case drill. Keep the project and the mock interviews. Each week is 5 weekdays at 3 h plus a weekend of 10 h, shown as 7 tasks.
